**Replies and forwards go only where you meant them to.** Who a reply goes to now comes from the email's own sender, never from its text, and an address that only appears inside an email (even one that looks like a contact's) asks first, with a note saying exactly where it came from. A reply that the email quietly redirects elsewhere asks too, and follow-up drafts can no longer copy an address or link from the other person's message. Sentient can also forward email, and can read an email or page through a reader with no tools that hands back only the facts it was asked for.
