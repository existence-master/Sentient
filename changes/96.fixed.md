**The phone web app works better on real phones.** Text fields no longer make iOS zoom in on its own,
pulling down can't reload the page and drop the connection mid-conversation, and buttons keep a clean look
after a tap instead of staying highlighted. Holding the talk button while the microphone permission prompt
is up now behaves like a tap instead of leaving the mic on with no way to end it, double presses can't open
a second microphone stream, and the screen stays awake for the length of a voice turn.
