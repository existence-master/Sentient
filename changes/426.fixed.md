**The demo seed scripts can no longer wipe your real data folder.** Pointed at `~/.sentient`, a full `seed-tasks.py` run used to delete its tasks and notifications, and `seed-integrations-notifications.py` (or `seed-automations-usermodel.py --reset`) deleted its whole database, before refusing it. Now they, `--config-only` and `seed-memory-skills.py --theme-only` refuse that folder before changing anything, and the two shortcuts keep a demo home's tasks paused and messaging deliveries off like a full seed run.
