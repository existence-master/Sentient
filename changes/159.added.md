**Give Sentient a task by forwarding an email.** Turn on Tasks by email in Settings > Tasks and pick your Sentient address (for example you+sentient@gmail.com). Forward or copy an email to it and Sentient plans a task from it, which waits for your OK. Only the people you allow can do this, checked by their real email address and never by the name shown next to it, and anything Sentient would send because of the email still asks you first, saying where each address came from.
