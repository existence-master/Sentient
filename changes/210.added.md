**Find skills other people wrote in the new Skills hub.** Skills now has a "Find skills" tab that lists skills from Anthropic's and OpenAI's public collections, or from a GitHub repository you add. Each one shows who made it and what it asks for, and you can preview it with its safety check before you install. A skill you install waits in Pending review and is pinned to the exact version you saw; updates show what changed and wait for your review again.
