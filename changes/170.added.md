**You can always see when a microphone or camera is on.** A red indicator appears in the title bar and on the tray icon within a second of any microphone or camera turning on, on this computer or on your phone or glasses. Click it to see which device and what for, and turn it off. Settings > Voice now says plainly where your voice goes, and the glasses firmware gains a camera-off mode that only the glasses can end, with the camera light wired to the camera's power.
