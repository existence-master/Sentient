**MCP apps are now kept apart from your own words.** When an app asks Sentient a question, Sentient treats its words as the app's, never as yours: anything that sends, deletes or runs code asks you first, in the window and as a notification you can answer on your phone, and by default a memory the app states waits in Review instead of being saved as something you said (the one opt-out is the Integrations setting that lets MCP memories skip Review, `integrations.mcp_server_memories_skip_review`). Apps also get a key of their own that opens only the few things the tools need, never your settings or approvals, and you can replace it under Integrations to cut off every app holding the old one. Before that key is sent anywhere, the server makes whatever answers at the engine's address prove it holds the same key, with the proof tied to that address (host and port) so it cannot be relayed from the real engine, and it checks again before every call, following the engine when it restarts on a new port. An app's name is also cleaned and framed before it is shown to you ("An app called ..."), so an app cannot name itself something that reads like a message from Sentient where you decide whether to allow it. An app also cannot shape its turns: files, a model of its choice and the incognito switch in its requests are ignored.
