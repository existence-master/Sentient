**MCP apps are now kept apart from your own words.** When an app asks Sentient a question, Sentient treats its words as the app's, never as yours: anything that sends, deletes or runs code asks you first, in the window and as a notification you can answer on your phone, and a memory the app states waits in Review instead of being saved as something you said. Apps also get a key of their own that opens only the few things the tools need, never your settings or approvals, and you can replace it under Integrations to cut off every app holding the old one.
